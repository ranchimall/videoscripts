## 🎬 How to Use for Video Production

### Option A: Export Full Video (MP4 / WebM) with Custom Timings
1. Click the gold **"🎬 Export Video"** button in the top bar.
2. In the Video Exporter window:
   - Set the exact duration (e.g. `5s`, `3s`, `8.5s`) for each mathematician.
   - Choose your format (**MP4** or **WebM**) and transition style (**Smooth Cross-Dissolve** or **Instant Cut**).
   - Choose your framerate (**30 FPS** or **60 FPS**).
3. Click **"▶ Start Video Export"** — watch the real-time live preview monitor and progress bar encode your video.
4. The final 1080p video file downloads automatically to your computer!

### Option B: Export Individual High-Res PNG Frames
- Click **"📸 Export PNG"** (or press <kbd>S</kbd>) to download an ultra-sharp 4K/1080p snapshot of any slide to drop into Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

### Option C: Live Browser Presentation & Recording
- Use <kbd>→</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> and <kbd>←</kbd> to cycle between slides smoothly in fullscreen mode (<kbd>F11</kbd>).

## 🛠️ Customizing Text, Images & Adding Mathematicians

1. Press <kbd>E</kbd> or click **"Edit & Script"** on the bottom bar.
2. In the Creator Studio Drawer, you can:
   - Edit any mathematician's name, lifespan, nationality, institution, and breakthrough title.
   - Enter a custom image URL or click the **Upload icon** to pick a photo from your computer.
   - Edit the 3 contribution highlight cards and the LaTeX equation.
   - Click **"Apply to Current Frame"** for instantaneous live preview.
   - Click **"+ Add New"** to add more mathematicians to your episode.
   - Click **"Export Deck JSON"** to save your script to a file, and reload it later via **"Import Deck JSON"**.
